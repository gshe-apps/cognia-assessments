# Cognia Assessment Hub, Admin Wireframes

Interactive wireframes for the Summer 2027 Assessment Hub, exported from the Claude Design canvas.

## Structure

- `project/canvas.json`, canvas layout, screen titles and flow labels
- `project/*.dc.html`, one file per screen
- `project/cognia-skin.css` and `project/ds/cognia/`, shared styles and design tokens

## Flows

**System Administrator, district oversight**
- Interim: SA 1 Home, SA 2 Overview, SA 3 Analytics, SA 4 Follow-up, SA 6 Assessments
- Formative: SA 5 Formative
- Coming soon: Screeners, Alt Assessments, Data & Reporting

**School Administrator, building-level review**
- Interim: School 1 Home, School 2 Overview, School 3 Analytics, School 4 Follow-up, School 6 Assessments
- Formative: School 5 Formative
- Coming soon: Screeners, Alt Assessments
